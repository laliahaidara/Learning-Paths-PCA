\section{Complementary Experiment: The GRASP Metaheuristic}\label{sec:grasp}

MAS-Cloud+ \cite{mendes2022mascloud,mendes2024mascloud}, an architecture from the research group of Prof.\ Marotta, provisions virtual machines in the cloud with three reasoning models: fast heuristic rules, a metaheuristic (GRASP) and formal optimization. Our comparison covered every design technique except the metaheuristic. This section fills the gap by adapting the GRASP of MAS-Cloud+ to the learning-path domain (module \code{algorithms/grasp.py}, Algorithm~\ref{alg:grasp}; design in Section~\ref{sec:design-extra}, analysis in Section~\ref{sec:complexity-grasp}). The adaptation uses a fixed seed, so every run is reproducible, and the same operation counter as the other algorithms. The experiments are implemented in \code{experiment\_grasp.py}.

\subsection{Quality on random instances}

On the 1,800 random instances of the correctness protocol (three families, 40 seeds, five values of $\rho$ and $n\in\{8,12,16\}$) GRASP found the optimum in 98.1\% of the cases, with a mean gap of 0.044\% and a worst gap of 9.4\%. Table~\ref{tab:grasp-families} compares it with the pure greedy on the three families for $\rho=0.3$ and 30 instances per cell; the seeds differ from those of Section~\ref{sec:exp-greedy}, which is why the worst greedy gap for the weakly correlated family with $n=8$ appears here as 25.0\% and there as 25.9\%.

\subsection{Comparison with the exact DP}

On the topic instance ($n=78$) GRASP costs 4.5 times more than the exact DP (211,950 against 46,878 operations) and still loses up to 3.2\%, more than the greedy itself on the mastery score (2.3\%). On the module instance ($n=12$) GRASP costs about half of the DP operations (3,480 to 3,616 against 7,212), with gaps from 0\% to 1.5\%. At both levels, only the DP guarantees the optimum.

This goes in the same direction as what MAS-Cloud+ reports in its own domain: in the preprint \cite{mendes2022mascloud}, the formal-optimization model outperformed the metaheuristic one by 19.27\% in performance with 6.53\% less monetary cost (we quote the preprint figures; the journal version \cite{mendes2024mascloud} should be consulted for the final numbers). In both domains, when the instance fits the exact method, the exact method is the best choice; the metaheuristic is the alternative for when it does not fit, and with the small $T$ of study paths that case did not appear in any tested instance.
